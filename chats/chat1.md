# Birthday site for Ibro

_Started 2026-10-02 19:19 UTC_

---

## User

PROJECT: Birthday site for Ibrahim ("Ibro")
CONCEPT: "The Ibro story so far." A short, warm tribute to who he is,
told in chapters. Anyone who opens it should understand it and smile,
whether or not they follow football.

TONE
Warm and celebratory first, lightly funny, sincere at the end.

COLOUR (taken from him, not from a club)
- Base: near-black #0B0B0D
- Primary: warm gold #E8B84A (his own kit trim)
- Surface: soft charcoal #17171B for cards
- Text: off-white #F5F2EA
- Chapter accent: Liverpool red #C8102E, used only in the football chapter
All colours are theme variables so they can be changed later.

TYPOGRAPHY
- Display: a confident serif (Fraunces or Playfair Display) for his
  name and chapter titles
- Body: a clean sans (DM Sans) for the letter and captions
- His name is the biggest thing on the site

HERO
His photo (cropped to him alone) or the standing avatar, large and
centred, with "Happy Birthday, Ibro" and the line "Today is about you."
Gold confetti falls once, then stops.

AVATAR (flat illustration, bold outlines, simple features)
Tall, slim, athletic build; short low cut; clean-shaven; deep brown skin.
Two outfits: everyday clothes (default) and his black and gold kit
(football chapter only). Poses: standing, waving, celebrating.

SECTIONS
1. Hero: him, his name, the date
2. Candles: a cake, "Make a wish, Ibro"
3. The Ibro we know: three or four cards, one word each with a short
   line beneath (for example Loyal, Driven, Funny, Calm)
4. The journey: a vertical timeline of chapters. Football is one of them.
   Suggested chapters: The friend / The player / The grind / What's next
5. The letter: the emotional centre, calm and uncluttered
6. Closing: one wish for the year ahead, "From Sono", replay

MOTION
Gentle throughout. Sections fade and rise on scroll, candles flicker,
confetti appears only at the hero and after the candles. No games.

COPY VOICE
Plain, warm and personal. No commentary or football metaphors
outside the football chapter.
